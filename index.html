Welcome

Welcome to the official home of Leithinis. Step inside to discover the latest releases and stories behind the music.

Leithinis is the Irish word for “peninsula”, and it’s a fitting name for us. We are Michael Sidwell and Alan Patterson, two musicians living on the beautiful Ards Peninsula in County Down, Northern Ireland. The place we call home is the heart of everything we create. Our debut album, Sky Full of Stars, is coming soon. Its first single, “Wolves”, was released in July 2026 and is already being played on radio stations across the region, including BBC Radio Ulster. A song of aching loss, it explores the memories that haunt us and intrude on the present. Its melody is beautiful and evocative, and its lyrics cut straight to the heart. Leithinis make rich, inventive and original music. Our lyrics probe the matters of the heart and explore the role memory plays in healing. Drawing on jazz, folk and classical influences, we weave together contemporary and classical instruments to craft melodies that hold your attention, with musical surprises along the way to keep you listening until the final note. Alan Patterson has released three critically acclaimed albums and an EP. Michael Sidwell writes songs with memorable melodies and searching lyrics. His poetry is finding a growing audience, and he is currently writing a trilogy of children’s books rooted in Irish mythology. Our music is inspired by the landscape and light of the place we live. It isn’t loud or brash, but lyrical, tuneful and intricate. We hope you’ll give it a listen.
