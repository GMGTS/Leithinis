Welcome

Welcome to the official home of Leithinis. Step inside to discover the latest releases and stories behind the music.

Leithinis is Irish for Peninsula. We are Michael Sidwell, and Alan Patterson. We live on the beautiful Ards Peninsula in the County of Down, Northern Ireland. We are inspired by the place we live. Our first album entitled, "Sky Full Of Stars" will be released shortly. A single from the album, "Wolves" was released in July 2026, and is being played on a wide range of Radio Stations, including BBC Radio Ulster. A story of aching loss, and memories that haunt and invade our present, "Wolves", has a beautiful and evocative melody, and lyrics that tear at your soul. Leithinis create rich and inventive melodies that are truly original. Set to lyrics that probe and dissect important matters of the heart, and the importance of memory to healing. Combining jazz, folk and classical influences, Leithinis use both contemporary and classical instruments to create melodies that hold our attention, and throw musical surprises into the mix that engage the listener to the end. Alan Patterson has previously released 3 albums, and an EP, which have been critically acclaimed. Michael Sidwell writes songs with memorable melodies and searching lyrics. Michael's poetry is also gaining a growing audience, and he is currently writing a trilogy of children's books rooted in Irish mythology. The music of Leithinis is inspired by the beauty of the place where we live. It doesn’t shout or roar; instead it’s lyrical, tuneful, and intricate.
