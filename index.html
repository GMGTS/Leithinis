Leithinis is Irish for Peninsula. We are Michael Sidwell, and Alan Patterson. We live on the beautiful Ards Peninsula in the County of Down, Northern Ireland. We are inspired by the place we live. Our first album entitled, Sky Full Of Stars will be released shortly.

A single from the album, Wolves was released in July 2026, and is being played on a wide range of Radio Stations, including BBC Radio Ulster. A story of aching loss, and memories that haunt and invade our present, Wolves, has a beautiful and evocative melody, and lyrics that tear at your soul. Leithinis create rich and inventive melodies that are truly original. Set to lyrics that probe and dissect important matters of the heart, and the importance of memory to healing.

Combining jazz, folk and classical influences, Leithinis use both contemporary and classical instruments to create melodies that hold our attention, and throw musical surprises into the mix that engage the listener to the end. Alan Patterson has previously released 3 albums, and an EP, which have been critically acclaimed. Michael Sidwell writes songs with memorable melodies and searching lyrics. Michael's poetry is also gaining a growing audience, and he is currently writing a trilogy of children's books rooted in Irish mythology. The music of Leithinis is inspired by the beauty of the place where we live. It doesn’t shout or roar; instead it’s lyrical, tuneful, and intricate.

Alan Patterson

Alan Patterson has released three albums and an EP as a solo artist.

His debut album, Damaged But Not Broken (2015), is a collection of beautiful songs reflecting on memories of home, love, and the perils of long hours spent working for an ungrateful employer. It was followed in 2016 by the EP The Journey, which turns toward hope and resolution. Dancing With Strangers (2018) is an album crammed with fine songs and playing, while If Sam Wore Pink and Other Stories (2020) is a mainly instrumental collection.
Alan's solo work crosses many genres, drawing on folk, jazz, classical and country influences. Real instruments and wonderfully talented players, set within superb arrangements and finished to a high production standard, are the hallmark of his solo recordings.
